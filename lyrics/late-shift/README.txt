Drop your own lyrics in here.

One song per file, .txt or .md, in the folder of the channel that should sing it. The file's first
line can be the title; everything else is the song. Section tags in square brackets work best:

    Ocean Avenue

    [Verse 1]
    There's a place off Ocean Avenue
    ...
    [Chorus]
    ...

A channel whose "lyrics" setting is "files" only sings what is in here. On "mixed" it uses your
files first and then writes its own. On "invent" it writes everything itself and never reads here.

Nothing in here is ever deleted by the app, and a file is only used once per channel.
