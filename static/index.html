Covers need "Learn from real songs" switched on in Settings.
A real recording, its music kept, one of our voices singing it. The AI singer is YuE2's own voice, lifted from its own songs, male or female to match the original; at 0% nothing of your recording reaches the model. Pick AI singer in the voice list to use it outright. For any other singer, right-click a song in the Library and Make a voice from this singer.
The vocal is lifted out, sung again in that voice and mixed back over the original music; the original singer is gone either way. The % is your trained voice against the AI singer it was trained from: less of you is the AI's steadier delivery with your timbre pulled in. Pitch "auto" moves the melody by whole octaves into the voice's range so the key stays with the band; "as the song is sung" leaves it where the original sits. The take lands in My Songs next to the song, with the new vocal alone under "vocals". For a song already in the Library: open it and use Re-voice.

How channels work

          
A channel is a sound (its style lines, picked at random and, when it has a sound set, crossed with it about half the time) plus the themes its songs are about. Tune in plays it here; the Dial tab is the big-button version for the car. Make one cooks a song right now, Edit changes the sound, the themes, the mood and the bans (type a theme of your own into the list and it becomes one). Auto stock keeps five unplayed songs ready on every channel.

How the line works

          
One song renders at a time, about three minutes each. Your own songs (Create, Make one, re-voice) go ahead of the radio's automatic restocking. Every station keeps five unplayed songs ready; the station being listened to is restocked first. Cancel removes a song from the line; cancelling the one being made starts the next one. Opening this tab never interrupts playback.
